I’m a master’s student at the Bren School of Environmental Science & Management at UC Santa Barbara, specializing in Water Resource Management. My work sits at the intersection of spatial data science, climate-smart agriculture, and conservation planning — with a particular focus on using satellite remote sensing and geospatial modeling to understand how land and water systems are changing. Before Bren, I worked in sustainability communications in New York and spent time farming, volunteering on kiwi conservation in New Zealand, and getting my hands dirty in as many ecosystems as possible.

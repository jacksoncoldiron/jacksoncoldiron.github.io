Current Research

Agrivoltaic & Agroforestry Suitability in Sub-Saharan Africa

Arnhold Environmental Graduate Fellow — SPARCLE Project | Conservation International + Ashley Larsen Lab, UCSB | Oct 2025–Present

Developing and executing a multi-criteria decision analysis for agrivoltaics and agroforestry suitability across Sub-Saharan Africa, integrating 10+ climate, soil, land cover, and socioeconomic criteria to identify optimal multifunctional landscapes under current conditions and future climate scenarios. Also quantifying food and energy co-benefits by estimating crop yield response under shading and agroforestry timber/fruit/nut outputs for suitable lands.

Methods: R, Google Earth Engine, QGIS, Quarto Data: CHELSA climate, MODIS LULC, SRTM DEM, WDPA protected areas, SoilGrids

Remote Sensing of Irrigation & Drought

Independent Research | UCSB Geography — Advisor: Zoe Pierrat | Aug 2025–Present

Isolating the human contribution to evapotranspiration to reveal irrigation-driven humidity impacts across Midwestern croplands. Integrates modeled and observed ET data (ECOSTRESS & NLDAS) to assess atmosphere feedbacks, and uses solar-induced chlorophyll fluorescence (SIF) anomalies and lagged ET correlations to detect where irrigation sustains yields while amplifying heat extremes.

Methods: Python (xarray, rasterio, numpy, matplotlib), Google Earth Engine Data: ECOSTRESS, NLDAS, NASA GEOS-CF SIF

Climate Risk & Indigenous Territories in Amazonia

Data Manager, Master’s Group Project | Conservation International — IPLC Center | Apr 2025–Present Client: Sushma Shrestha (CI) | Faculty Advisor: Bruce Kendall

Partnering with CI’s Global Indigenous Peoples and Local Communities (IPLC) Center to assess how climate risks intersect with IPLC territories across Amazonia and their implications for carbon storage and biodiversity. Using ArcGIS Pro and R to identify specific territories suitable for Nature-based Solutions intervention based on climate vulnerability, natural asset portfolios, and territorial rights.

Methods: ArcGIS Pro (ModelBuilder, Spatial Analyst), R Data: IPCC climate projections, RAISG IPLC boundaries, Global Forest Watch

Mapping Dry-Season Irrigation in Zambia

Graduate Research Assistant | Water, Vegetation & Society (WAVES) Lab, UCSB | Nov 2024–Present

Created a novel satellite image labeling protocol using Google Earth Pro to build a benchmark machine learning dataset mapping dry-season irrigation across all of Zambia over the past 10 years. Managed a team of 4 undergraduate researchers to label 2,325 images at 933 randomly selected locations; irrigation was detected in 27% of images.

Methods: Google Earth Pro, R, Python Data: Sentinel-2, Landsat imagery

Stomatal Conductance of Coast Live Oaks

Field Volunteer Researcher | Water, Vegetation & Society (WAVES) Lab, UCSB | Jan 2025–Present

Measuring stomatal conductance of Coast Live Oaks at Dangermond Preserve to assess plant physiological responses to drought, contributing to the Earth Research Institute’s mission to predict ecosystem resilience.

Past Research & Projects

Climate-Smart Agriculture in Zambia

Research Intern | University of Zambia — Lusaka, Zambia | Jun–Aug 2025

Conducted geospatial statistical analysis revealing 400 km² of previously undocumented small-scale dry-season irrigation across Zambia. Developed a Shiny app to make findings accessible to local stakeholders and policymakers, and held 10 meetings with Zambian stakeholders to gather technical and policy feedback on applications ranging from solar pump monitoring to national irrigation data gaps.

Methods: R (sf, terra, shiny), ArcGIS Pro

Redshanks Habitat & Fuel Reduction Impact Analysis

Conservation Intern | Los Padres ForestWatch — Santa Barbara, CA | Dec 2024–Jun 2025

Conducted GIS habitat and vegetation analyses evaluating ecological impacts of proposed Forest Service fuel reduction projects, producing spatial data layers to support conservation-focused alternatives. Analyzed Adenostoma sparsifolium (Redshanks) distribution in ArcGIS Pro to assess environmental and climatic habitat characteristics for climate adaptation planning.

Methods: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS

Salinas River Watershed — Groundwater Sustainability Plan

UCSB Bren School | Sep–Dec 2025 | View Report (PDF)

Conducted an integrated watershed management analysis synthesizing climate, hydrologic, land-use, population, and water-use datasets to produce a groundwater sustainability management plan for the Salinas River Watershed. Developed recommendations integrating recharge enhancement, irrigation efficiency analysis, and land-use-specific BMPs to reduce overdraft and support SGMA compliance.

Methods: R, ArcGIS Pro Data: DWR CASGEM, PRISM climate, USGS streamflow

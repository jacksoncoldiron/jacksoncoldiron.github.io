## Tips
- **Shape:** landscape (wider than tall), ideally at least 1600 px wide. Claude crops to a consistent 3:2 frame, so
  keep the important part near the center.
- **What works best:** a map or figure from the analysis, a screenshot of the app, or a field photo of the site.
- **Captions and credits (optional):** add a `captions.txt` here with one line per image, e.g.
  `zambia: Center-pivot and smallholder plots near Mkushi, Sentinel-2 true color (2023)`.
- Missing images are fine. Those cards just stay text-only.
